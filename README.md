# WIP, Billing and Revenue Recognition Tracker (Learning Project)

> **Self-directed learning project. Projects, clients, hours, rates and invoices are fictional.** This is a simplified illustration of work-in-progress concepts. It is not accounting work, and accounting treatment decisions belong to an accountant.

## What is inside
| Sheet | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| `Tracker` | Per project: billable and non-billable hours, billable labour and expenses, revenue earned to date, invoiced, unbilled WIP, billed in excess, paid, outstanding AR, and flags |
| `Glossary` | 19 plain-English terms with where each appears and whether to handle or escalate |
| `Projects`, `Time_Costs`, `Billing` | Five fictional contracts (3 time and materials, 2 fixed price), 48 hour and expense lines, 6 invoices |
| `Escalation` | 13 situations with what I can do myself, who I would escalate to, and what I would bring |

## Method (simplified)
Time and materials: revenue earned = billable hours x rate + billable expenses. Fixed price: revenue earned = (billable hours / budget hours) x contract value, capped at 100%. Unbilled WIP = revenue earned minus invoiced when positive. Billed in excess = invoiced minus revenue earned when positive. The real method for a real contract is the accountant's decision.

## Results on the fictional data
Revenue earned $266,500, invoiced $233,400, unbilled WIP $41,500, billed in excess $8,400, paid $132,000, outstanding AR $101,400. Three self-checks show OK (revenue = invoiced + unbilled - billed in excess; invoiced ties to the billing sheet; hours tie to the time sheet). Flags show one project never invoiced, one billed ahead of work, one near budget hours, and one invoice unpaid 30+ days.
